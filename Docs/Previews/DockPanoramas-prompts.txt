共同绘制要求
Game texture: ultrawide panoramic hand-painted theatrical backcloth, a single continuous horizontal landscape painting, frontal/elevation of distant scenery for a miniature wooden theatre. Compose as an extra wide landscape, aspect 5:1 or wider if possible. For fitting a cylindrical band keep the entire recognizable skyline, features and horizon in the narrow middle 27% of image height; outside that band only atmospheric sky above and receding ground/water below. All the way across include left, middle and right scenery, no division, no repeating panels, no vignette. Broad broken gouache brushstrokes on scenic canvas, softly distressed pigments and edges, stylised late 19th century dark fantasy harbour. Restrained contrast so foreground actors will read. No text, panels, frame, ribbons, pillars, curtains, stage, ceiling or audience. Deliver illustration edge to edge.
